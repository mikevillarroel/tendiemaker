TendieMaker — FRESH START (v0.8-ready)
====================================

This folder is a complete, ready-to-open Xcode project. No File > New > Project,
no dragging files in, no deleting duplicates.

STEPS (5 minutes):

1. (Optional) Delete the old "TendieMaker" folder from your Mac to keep it clean.

2. Double-click  TendieMaker.xcodeproj   to open it in Xcode.

3. In the left sidebar, click the BLUE "TendieMaker" icon at the very top,
   then the "Signing & Capabilities" tab, and under "Team" pick your
   Apple ID team. (Xcode > Settings > Apple Accounts to add it, if needed.)

4. Plug in your iPhone with a cable. At the top of the Xcode window,
   next to the Run (play) button, pick your iPhone as the destination.

5. Press Run.

HOW TO KNOW YOU HAVE THE NEW ONE:
Open the app on your iPhone and look at the very bottom of the screen.
It must say:  App version: v0.8-ready

If it does NOT say that, the old build is still on your phone:
delete the TendieMaker app from the phone first, then press Run again.

WHAT'S NEW IN v0.8-ready:
- Same HDR frame-extraction fix as v0.7-hdr (this is the version that
  should stop the black-screen tendies).
- The app now RESETS ITSELF after a build finishes: once you close the
  share sheet, the form goes back to the start, ready for the next video.
- The build runs fully in the background, so typing a name stays smooth.

NOTE: your phone may now show TWO "TendieMaker" icons (old + new).
Delete the old one from the phone.

THEN TEST:
Pick Video > choose one > pick a mode > Build Tendie.
Watch the "Progress" section: it shows the first extracted frame.
If the preview is BLACK, the problem is frame extraction on that video -
tell Jarvis what the status text says (e.g. "First frame: 1170x2532").
